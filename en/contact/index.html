How to Get Here

By Car

A4 motorway (Milan–Venice), exit Seriate or Nembro, then follow the SP35 towards Gazzaniga–Orezzo. About 25–30 minutes from the motorway.

Driving directions (Google Maps)

By Public Transport

Check connections from Bergamo at bergamotrasporti.it. Valle Seriana is served by SAB bus lines.
