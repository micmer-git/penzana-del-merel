Authentic Wood-Fired Pizza in the Italian Alps near Bergamo

La Penzana del Merel is an authentic Italian pizzeria and traditional restaurant in the heart of Val Seriana, just 24 km from Bergamo, Italy. Our wood-fired oven has been burning since 1959, crafting artisan pizzas and serving classic Bergamo cuisine in the stunning setting of the Italian Alps.

Best Pizza near Bergamo — Family-Run Since 1959

Looking for the best pizza near Bergamo? La Penzana del Merel in Orezzo offers genuine wood-fired pizza, homemade casoncelli (traditional Bergamo stuffed pasta), polenta taragna, grilled meats and house desserts. We're a family-run Italian mountain restaurant beloved by locals and visitors alike.

Where to Eat in Val Seriana — Bergamo Province

If you're exploring the Val Seriana or visiting Bergamo and its surroundings, La Penzana del Merel is a must-visit. Located in the village of Orezzo at 670m altitude, surrounded by Alpine scenery, our restaurant is the perfect stop for an authentic Italian dining experience away from tourist traps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is La Penzana del Merel located?

We're at Via alla Chiesa 4, Orezzo (Gazzaniga), in the Val Seriana valley, Bergamo province, Italy. Just 24 km from Bergamo city centre and easily reachable from Bergamo Airport (Orio al Serio).

Do you have a wood-fired oven?

Yes! Our traditional wood-fired oven has been the heart of La Penzana since 1959. Every pizza is baked in the wood-fired oven for an authentic, crispy taste you won't find elsewhere.

What traditional dishes do you serve?

Besides our famous wood-fired pizzas, we serve casoncelli alla bergamasca (traditional Bergamo stuffed pasta), polenta taragna, grilled meats, and homemade desserts. A true taste of Bergamo mountain cuisine.

Can I order takeaway pizza?

Yes! You can order takeaway through our online menu and pick up your pizza hot from the oven. We also deliver to nearby areas in the Val Seriana valley.

Is La Penzana del Merel a good restaurant for tourists visiting Bergamo?

Absolutely! If you're looking for an authentic Italian dining experience outside the city, La Penzana del Merel offers genuine Bergamo cuisine in a beautiful Alpine village setting, just 30 minutes from Bergamo. Many international visitors describe it as one of the best pizza restaurants near Bergamo.
